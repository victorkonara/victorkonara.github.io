# Blurb copied from the Pan Macmillan product page (source of truth).
blurb: |
  A former world-class sorcerer (now a cat) and a disgraced paladin must earn their freedom. Together, they'll con their way to freedom – through every daemon, princess and tourist they can find . . .

  Art used to be human. Not just any human: a world-class Ritualist, Professor at the University of Singhapura, a sorcerer of some distinction. But when he crosses the wrong people, he finds himself stuck at the edge of civilization in the body of a fat, orange cat in debt to a local duchess.

  His only ticket back to opposable thumbs is Gary the Paladin – a master swordsman and musical prodigy, with a jaw you could crack coconuts on. Unfortunately for Art, Gary has zero street smarts and a heart of gold.

  Art and Gary will have to hustle bandits, endure exorcisms and outrun iron-fisted landlords. But when they stumble into proper danger, Art realizes that survival may require more than cunning . . .

  It may require being friends with someone who can't read.

  Terry Pratchett meets Dungeon Crawler Carl and Puss in Boots in this hilarious Dungeons & Dragons-inspired fantasy novel.
